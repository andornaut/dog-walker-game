Dog Walker

A cozy pixel-art lane runner. Walk a pack of dogs through the neighbourhood, and every bump in the road turns into a choice.

Play ← → to switch lanes, or tap either side

The idea

Cozy, not frantic

A calm pace and gentle failure. You keep what you earn.

Every bump is a story

Hit a skunk or a puddle and pick a card: pay up or upset the owners.

Charming chaos

Cute dogs, dramatic owners and a silly neighbourhood.
