The idea

Cozy, not frantic

A calm pace and gentle failure. You keep what you earn.

Every bump is a story

Hit a skunk or a puddle and pick a card: pay up, or upset the owners.

Charming chaos

Cute dogs, dramatic owners and a silly neighbourhood.

The cast

Pick a walkerCasey, Ada, Ben or Trista. All four are free from the first walk.

Nine dogs to walkEach has an owner to keep happy and a trait that changes how a walk goes. The outlined three are the starters; the rest join from the shop.

The first clientsThe starters' owners are laid-back, for an easier first run. On a new save they wait at the gate for the first walk.

Every bump is a choice

The pack walks into a skunk. Each dog rolls to be caught: Biscuit and Koda are sprayed, and small Duchess slips past. The card's costs cover the two caught dogs. Pick one and see how it lands with the owners.

On the road

5 encountersHit one and a card opens.

4 moving hazardsA warning sign shows at the lane edge about a second before each one moves. The squirrel and loose dog open their encounter cards; the ball and cyclist are minor bumps.

3 lucky findsGood things to bump into.

6 forced stopsOne per zone, and you can't dodge it.

7 minor obstaclesNo card: the pack stumbles and drops $2 in tips. Barriers stand only on Woof Way and Sniff Street Shops.

Build your kennel

The home screen is the shop, in three steps: choose your walker, choose your dogs, choose gear (optional), then start the walk. Hold a dog or gear item for its detail card.

Dog slotsStart with one; buy two more.

GearBought once; take up to one piece on each walk.

  • Fanny pack
  • Whistle
  • Poop bags
  • Running shoes
  • Lucky collar

Achievements15 badges in four groups, on the Stats page's badge wall. Locked ones are greyed with their goal.
