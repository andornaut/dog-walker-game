The idea

Cozy, not frantic

A calm pace and gentle failure. You keep what you earn.

Every bump is a story

Hit a skunk or a puddle and pick a card: pay up or upset the owners.

Charming chaos

Cute dogs, dramatic owners and a silly neighbourhood.

The cast

Pick a walkerAda, Ben, Trista or Casey. All four are free from the first walk.

Nine dogs to walkEach has an owner to keep happy and a trait that changes how a walk goes. The outlined three are the starters; the rest join from the shop.
