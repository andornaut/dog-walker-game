# Next

Everything set aside for later. Nothing here is scheduled, and no order or version is implied. Undecided ideas live in [`ideas.md`](ideas.md); the current plan lives in [`game-design.md`](game-design.md).

## Worlds

The game grows sideways rather than upward. A world is a set of 4 zones with its own theme, obstacles and even mechanics: e.g. fantasy, the future, the past, dinosaurs. Maple Street is the first world and the only one in the current plan. Finishing a full run in a world shows the next one on the home screen; buying access lets you walk it. Stats already track the best zone reached and times each world was finished.

## Other ideas for later

- Dog training: two courses per dog you own: Heel (the dog pulls half as often, $100) and Good manners (its owner starts with +1 ♥ and has +1 max ♥, $150)
- Walker kit (permanent, always on): Quick feet (lane changes 20% faster, $300); Treat belt (carry 4 treats, $300); Business cards (+$3 fee per dog per zone, $400); Good eye (the pull tell starts 0.5s earlier, $400)
- Preset walkers: 3 to 4 to pick from; the first is free, the rest $200 each as cosmetics
- Cosmetics: 2 accessories per dog, each made for that dog ($80 each; proposed: Duchess tiara and pearl collar, Nugget sunglasses and gold chain, Zippy racing goggles and sports jersey, Pierre beret and bow tie, Biscuit chef hat and scarf, Dash aviator cap and cape, Snowball flower crown and veil, Waffles firefighter hat and raincoat, Koda beanie and trail pack); 6 leash colours ($40 each); 6 home screen decor pieces with fixed spots in the gate scene ($60 to $160)
- iOS and Android app wrappers
- Zone encounter mixes: each zone weights the existing obstacles and stops differently (Woof Way: puddles and the mail carrier; Bark Park: other dogs and squirrels; Sniff Street Shops: thieves and the ice cream truck; The Long Way Home: everything, busier)
- Tip streak: each tip collected without a hit raises tip value a little; a hit resets it
- Owner requests: each owner asks for one small thing per walk (e.g. Rosa: "keep Duchess dry"), shown on the dog's slot; meeting it pays a bonus and a happy quip
- Walk of the day: each walk rolls one modifier shown before starting (e.g. rainy: more puddles, tips ×1.2; market day: more thieves and tips; quiet Sunday: fewer obstacles and tips)
- Minigames as an alternative way to resolve encounters
- Branching paths between zones: the player chooses the route
- Permanent unlocks as in-run choices: pick a run boost or a permanent unlock, Hades-style
- Special dogs found mid-walk, only with 2 or fewer dogs; once found, they join the roster
- Your own dog: an end-game unlock
- Time of day: dawn, midday, dusk, night
- Weather: variants with pros and cons, some mostly cosmetic
- Off-leash dog park: a zone unlocked by an upgrade
- House hub: a home base you decorate and improve with mostly cosmetic unlocks
- Art polish beyond a basic pixel pass; sound beyond a few effects and one music loop
- Accounts and cloud saves
