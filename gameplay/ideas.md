# Ideas

Undecided and cut ideas; nothing here is a commitment.

Format: `- [status] **Idea**: short description · notes`. Statuses: **floated** (said, not discussed), **exploring** (being discussed), **cut** (decided against, kept with the reason so it isn't argued again).

## Look and feel

- [floated] **Walk the sidewalk, not the road**: the pack walks a sidewalk, park path or boardwalk instead of the road, so the scene reads as a walk · pairs with Sidewalk lanes in [`next.md`](next.md)

## Cut

- [cut] **Dog energy, owner temperament and quirks**: per-dog rules beyond size · dogs differ by size only, owners only by their quips
- [cut] **Horizon and sky**: an Outrun-style horizon with sky at the top of the road · the backdrop band keeps its cross street
- [cut] **Mud encounter**: a mud obstacle · puddles cover it
- [cut] **Scripted first walk**: a one-neighbourhood tutorial walk with guaranteed encounters · first-time hints and the help screen teach the rules
- [cut] **Story recap on the end screen**: retell the walk's biggest moments · the end screen shows the walk's earnings and the owners' closing lines
- [cut] **Walker pace and style**: fast or slow walkers, charmers and hustlers · agility and one perk per walker
- [cut] **Thief whistle**: gear that makes thieves flee · thieves never flee
