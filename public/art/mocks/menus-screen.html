Menus

A proposal for the screens around a walk: the pause menu and settings over the dimmed road, the end screen at sunset, the Stats page with its Runs, Dogs and Badges tabs, and the badge celebration over the home screen. The end screen, Stats page and celebration follow the game's layout, with every text at 2x. Everything is drawn from the exported art and the two BMFonts. Bank, logbook, dog stats and badges are sample data; quips are the owners' real lines.

What's on it

  • Pause: the run frozen under the menu, the road dimmed one ramp step twice. A ui-panel with a 1px tan rim holds Resume, Help, Settings and Head home (ui-button), and Head home's note sits plum on a tan plate. The bar's pause button stays pressed while the menu is open.
  • Settings: Sound (mute all) with ui-switch, Music and Effects volume sliders, and Reduced motion. Every row is 44 tall and the whole row is the tap target. Muted, the sliders go inactive and read "Muted". Effects plays sfx-tip when you let go. The bar's sound button and the Sound row share one state.
  • End screen: on screen-end, the logo in the sky, the run's breakdown and total, zones walked as ui-zone-pips, the bank, and one closing line per owner in a ui-quip bubble: a happier line from owners still walking, the fired line (and fired face) from those who fired you. A new best drops the ui-new-best ribbon in, with fx-new-best bursting behind the panel. Badges the walk earned pop into a panel under it, each over fx-confetti, while the owners still walking hop. Back home is the primary ui-button.
  • Stats page: on screen-stats, three tabs: the open one in the selected state (ui-select, yellow face, lifted onto a shadow, ui-check tick), the others tan. Runs holds the overall figures, runs by outcome and the logbook; Dogs each dog's face with its walks, zones and firings. Badges is the wall on the wood, in two columns filled down in group order: each badge with its name (amber earned, cream locked) and goal (lightGrey), or its short goal where the full one does not fit. Tap a badge for its detail over the dimmed page: the badge at 2x, name, Earned or Locked, goal and line; an earned one bursts fx-confetti. Any tap closes it.
  • Home celebration: badges earned on launch or by a purchase, over the dimmed screen-home: the same badges panel, the bought dog's owner cheering under it, and a Nice! button.
  • Feedback: every tap sparkles (fx-tap) and pressed buttons sink into their lip. With reduced motion (the system setting, or the toggle here), the ribbon, pops and hops are skipped and each burst shows one still frame.

Art gaps

  • Detail confetti: fx-confetti is 64x64, so at 1x it sits wholly behind the 2x badge in the badge detail. The mock draws it at 2x; a larger burst would keep the specks 1px.
  • Selected tab: the ui-select band is the same yellow as the selected face, so on a tab the two merge into one wide yellow tile and the frame reads only by its ink lines.
