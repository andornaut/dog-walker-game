Menus

A proposal for the screens around a walk: the pause menu and settings over the dimmed road, the end screen at sunset, and the Stats page with the badge wall. Everything is drawn from the exported art and the two BMFonts. Bank, logbook and badges are sample data; quips are the owners' real lines.

What's on it

  • Pause: the run frozen under the menu, the road dimmed one ramp step twice. A ui-panel with a 1px tan rim holds Resume, Help, Settings and Head home (ui-button), and Head home's note sits plum on a tan plate. The bar's pause button stays pressed while the menu is open.
  • Settings: Sound (mute all) with ui-switch, Music and Effects volume sliders, and Reduced motion. Every row is 44 tall and the whole row is the tap target. Muted, the sliders go inactive and read "Muted". Effects plays sfx-tip when you let go. The bar's sound button and the Sound row share one state.
  • End screen: on screen-end, the logo in the sky, the run's breakdown, zones walked as ui-zone-pips, the bank, the ui-new-best ribbon pinned over the panel, and one closing line per owner in a ui-quip bubble: a happier line from owners still walking, the fired line (and fired face) from those who fired you.
  • Stats page: on screen-stats, the logbook numbers in a panel, then the badge wall in two columns, filled down in group order (firsts and mastery left, collection and silly right). Each badge shows its name, goal and line from the rulebook's Achievements table. Earned badges are in colour with an amber name; locked ones are greyed with a padlock and a cream name. Tap an earned badge for fx-confetti behind it; a locked one shakes.
  • Feedback: every tap sparkles (fx-tap) and pressed buttons sink into their lip. With reduced motion (the system setting, or the toggle here), the shakes and hops are skipped and the confetti shows one still frame.

  • End: panel 24,140 312x300 (the sky in screen-end). Logo 116,52. Ribbon 144,130. Breakdown labels x 40, values right at 196, rows y 182 to 218, total at 232. Zones and bank at x 212. Quip rows y 258, 298, 338: face well 36,row+1, bubble 80,row 244x36, tail 75,row+14. Button 48,384 264x40.
  • Stats: close button 332,10 (hit 316,0 44x44). Logbook panel 8,138 344x96, rows every 12 from y 162. Badge list: 2 columns at x 8 and 184 (each 168 wide, the tap target), 8 rows 46 tall from y 256. Badge at col,row+2; text column at col+36, 132 wide: name (font-title) at row+3, then goal and line (font-body) every 10 under it. Confetti at col-16,row-14.

Art gaps

  • Slider: no ui-slider track and knob; the volume sliders are drawn in code here.
  • Secondary button: ui-button has one amber face, so Resume can't stand out from Help, Settings and Head home. A tan secondary tag would match the scene's current colours.
  • Icons on cream: icon-sound and icon-music are drawn for the navy button face, so the Settings rows carry no icons. Effects and reduced motion have no icon at all.
