Start Screen

A proposal for the screen before a walk: bank and best walk on top, the walker and today's pack at the gate on Maple Street, then the dog picker, gear and logbook. Tap the dogs and gear to try it. The bank, logbook and quips are sample data.

Tap a dog to add or remove it (up to 3). Tap gear to equip it. Tap a locked dog or gear to buy it with the sample bank.

What's on it

  • Top bar: the bank, plus your best walk and walk count. It matches the run screen's bar.
  • Gate scene: the walker on the front path with today's pack, heading out to Maple Street. Picked dogs appear on their leashes, and their owner calls out a parting line.
  • Pack: all 9 dogs in a 3×3 grid, with size, trait, owner and temperament. Locked dogs show their price.
  • Gear: the 5 items, with one equipped. The equipped item's effect is spelled out below the row.
  • Logbook: best walk, totals and your last walk, written as a short story.
  • Start walk: shows the pack's fee per zone, and is disabled until a dog is picked. When ready it bobs gently; pressed, it sinks into its lip.
  • Feedback: every tap dips the tile and sparkles where you tapped. A picked dog or gear lifts onto a shadow, turns white, gets a pulsing amber frame and a green badge with its pick order, and its face hops; the dog also hops onto the path in a puff of dust. A locked item you can't afford shakes. With reduced motion, the highlights stay and the movement is skipped.

Open questions

  • Decided: the shop is in place (tap a locked dog or gear to buy it), the logbook shows what is mocked here, and equipped gear shows as a badge rather than on the walker.
  • Owner quips on pick: each owner needs a parting line in their temperament's voice.
  • Dogs without art: 6 dogs have no face yet, so locked ones show a silhouette and owned ones an initial.
