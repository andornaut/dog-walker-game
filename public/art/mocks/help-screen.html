Help Screen

A proposal for the in-game "How to walk" screen: six short pages, each with an illustration built from the real sprites. It opens from the start screen and the pause menu. The text is drafted from the rulebook in gameplay/game-design.md.

Tap Back or Next, the page dots, or use ← and →.

Pages

Design notes

  • Where it opens: a "?" button on the start screen and a "How to walk" item in the pause menu. The first walk also opens it once.
  • One idea per page: an illustration on top, a title, then at most four short lines. Pages turn with Back and Next, the dots or a swipe.
  • Real art: every illustration uses the exported sprites, so the help screen stays in step as art changes.
  • Text: drafted from the rulebook. The gameplay session owns the final wording.
