Color Test

The 360×640 run screen in the Endesga 32 palette, with 32×32 tiles and the agreed layout. The sprites are drawn in code to test colour roles, contrast and layout. They are not final art.

What to check

  • Lanes: are all 5 lanes readable at a glance in every zone?
  • Outlines: coloured outlines are the agreed style. Switch to black to see why: the skunk loses its silhouette.
  • Pip: the chihuahua is about 12 px wide at the back of the pack. Is it still readable?
  • Dog slots: the content fills only the top ~70 px of each 192 px slot. That leaves room for 48×48 faces or a full-body dog.
  • Dimming: palette remap keeps every pixel on the palette. 60% black adds off-palette pixels (see the counter).
  • Disabled card: grooming costs $15 × 3 dogs = $45 against $42, so it is greyed out. Turn on "Koda fired" to afford it.
  • Card art: these cards reuse the road sprites at 1×. The real cards get their own illustrations.
