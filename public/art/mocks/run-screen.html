Run screen

The new run screen as one reference for how the art fits together: the top bar with the state strip, the zone backdrop, the doghouse column and the pseudo-3D road with the pack, pickups, obstacles, moving hazards and every hit moment. Everything is drawn from the exported art and the two BMFonts; the pack, money and statuses are sample data.

What's on it

  • Top bar: ui-top-bar with the money and treat wells, the sound and pause ui-icon-buttons, the zone name, ui-zone-pips (the current pip fills amber as the zone goes by) and the state strip: icon-stinky while any dog is Stinky, an upgrade and the equipped gear. Tap a strip icon for its description in a ui-hint bubble.
  • Backdrop: backdrop-<zone>; its street's bottom row continues the projected road.
  • Dog column: a ui-doghouse per dog with the name, face-dog- and face-owner- faces by mood, the n/max count, ui-hearts in rows of six, and status icons (icon-stinky, icon-soaked, icon-spooked) beside the count while active. Tap a slot to give a treat (fx-heart up).
  • Road: camera B (horizon row -260, feet row 634, r 1.4, about 5.3s of road at 120 px/s). Each screen row samples one row of the 280x64 road-<zone> strip; outside the narrowing strip the backdrop's bottom row is extended, with scrolling lawn stripes and paving seams. Sprites scale in steps of 1x, 0.75x and 0.5x, using the @75/@50 variants where they exist and nearest-neighbour runtime scale otherwise, anchored bottom-centre on their lowest opaque row, over a shadow one ramp step darker than the road.
  • Pack: the walker at the feet row, three dogs in the game's formation, 1px leashes in the zone's leash colour. Walking plays walk; Standing stops the road and plays idle. Autopilot dodges obstacles and takes pickups; arrow keys, A/D or a tap on either half of the road steer by hand. Hitting something by hand plays its hit.
  • Encounter hits: 1s hit stop: fx-hit-<encounter> over the pack, ui-hit-flash-<colour> tiled down both screen edges (mirrored on the right), the label on ui-hit-label at 176x32, the dogs play shake if present (else react) and the walker stumble. Then the statuses: fx-status-<status> over the caught dogs and the icon on their slots. The skunk catches Koda and Biscuit, the puddle Duchess and Koda, the thief spooks everyone (sample catches). The card would open after the stop.
  • Minor bump: 0.6s stop: fx-bump where the pack meets the cone, "-$2 cone" in a ui-quip bubble with fx-coins under it, and fx-dizzy over the walker until the 0.5s lane lock ends.
  • Moving hazards: ui-warning and ui-warning-arrow at the lane edge 1s ahead, then squirrel-dash, loose-dog or ball-bounce cross (mirrored when moving left) or the cyclist rides down an outer lane faster than the road.
  • Zone switch: the next backdrop at once; the road-<zone>-start strip comes in from the far end and the new strip follows. Stinky clears when its zone ends. After The Long Way Home the mock wraps to Woof Way, which has no start strip.
  • Pause: everything freezes, the road dims two ramp steps, and icon-pause becomes icon-play on a pressed button. The pause menu itself is in the Menus mock.
  • Reduced motion: idles show frame 0, the stumble and the dogs' hops are skipped and the label does not drop in; bursts, flashes and dizzy stars still play.

Layout

  • Bar: coin 8,13, amount font-title 2x at 26,15; treat 104,13, count 122,18; sound button 146,10 (hit 124,0 44x44), pause 170,10 (hit 168,0 44x44); zone name right at 352,10; pips from 271,22 every 21; state strip icons from 214,17 every 18 (pause's hit area ends at 212).
  • Doghouses: 4,194 + 150 per slot. Name (36,11), dog face (20,36), owner face (8,78), count centred (56,90), owner (9,118), hearts from (9,127) every 9 across and 8 down. Status icons (48,97) then (48,73).
  • Road: s = F/(F+d), y = -260 + 894 s, F = 894/1.4. Lane centres x 108 + 56k at the feet. Pack: walker feet 634, dogs 58, 42 and 40 ahead at dx 0, -14, +14; leash from the hand (-11 or +10, 13 up) to the collar 14 up.
  • Hit moment: burst 64x64 centred on the dogs (top-left x-32, 546); plate 132,478 176x32 with the label's caps at row 489; flash strips at x 0 and 344, every 64 down.
  • Minor bump: burst centred on the pack's leading edge (d 80, y 535), midway between the walker's x and the obstacle's; quip bubble top 486; coins under it at 502; dizzy stars 32x16 at the walker's head, top 578.
  • Warnings: crossers: sign at the road edge on the entry side, bottom on the crossing row, arrow 17px inward. Cyclist: sign at the lane's far end (top 193), arrow under it rotated to point down.

Art gaps

  • No shake tag: no dog sprite has one, so the caught dogs fall back to react like the rest.
  • No down arrow: ui-warning-arrow points right and is only mirrored, so a cyclist coming down a lane has no arrow; here it is rotated 90 degrees in code.
  • Status icons have no place on the doghouse: the heart board is full width for a big dog (6 + 4 hearts), so the icons sit on the wall beside the owner window, one under and one over the count. A third status has no room.
  • Count overflows the doghouse: the n/max count centred at (56,90) in font-title is 23px for "7/10" and 29px for "10/10", but the wall between the owner window (ends at x 42) and the right edge (x 67) is 25px, so a big dog's count runs onto the dark edge and past the slot.
  • Two statuses on one dog: no rule for stacking fx-status-*; the mock alternates them every 1.2s.
  • Missing idles: other-dog has only walk, so a standing corgi on the road never rests.
  • Missing depth variants: no other-dog@75, squirrel@75, skunk-cloud@75, cyclist@75/@50 (documented as runtime scale), so those scale at runtime at 0.75x.
  • Parked car footprint: parked-car is a 40x80 billboard but its collision footprint is 3 rows (144 road px), which projects to about 160 screen rows near the pack: the car looks half as long as what it blocks.
  • Overlapping stink lines: the style guide keeps fx-stinky over the pack while Stinky lasts and also fx-status-stinky per dog; showing both doubles the lines, so only the per-dog status is drawn.
  • State strip against pause: pause's 44x44 hit area (168,0) reaches x 212, 18px into the zone well (194), so a strip starting at the well's left edge sits under pause's hit area. Here the strip starts at 214, which leaves room for three icons before the pips at 271; four would need the pips or the zone name to move.
  • Road verge: no art covers the ground beside the projected strip once it narrows below 280px; the mock extends the backdrop's bottom row.
