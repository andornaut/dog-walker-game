Walk a pack of dogs through Toronto. Every bump in the road turns into a choice.

A cozy pixel-art lane runner for your phone. Four neighbourhoods a walk, about 8 minutes.

Play

Drag or tap to steer, or use the arrow keys.

Every bump, a choice

The road moves: cars and trucks drive down their lanes, people step out and animals dart across. A plain bump costs a heart. Run into another dog, a skunk, a thief or a neighbourhood's own surprise and a card opens: pay up, lose hearts or take a debuff, with every outcome on the card. A puddle leaves the pack Muddy, and a cheap way out can bring Spotlight, Stinky or Zoomies, each for 30 seconds. Grab tips, treats, lost wallets and dropped gear on the way: found gear works for the rest of the walk.

Nine dogs to walk

Three small, three medium and three large. A small dog dodges 1 in 4 bumps, and each large dog slows your lane changes. Every dog pulls the pack a lane toward a squirrel it spots.

Keep the owners happy

Every dog comes with an owner and a row of hearts: 2 for a small dog, 3 for a medium one and 4 for a large one. A plain bump costs the owner of the dog that hit it a heart, and a treat a dog picks up gives its owner one back. At zero, the owner fires you and takes the dog home. Walk fewer dogs and your lead dog's owner starts with extra hearts.

Every walk starts in High Park, ends on Yonge Street and covers four of Toronto's six neighbourhoods. After each one, a celebration banks your pay, then the road forks: each path leads to a neighbourhood and carries an upgrade card, easiest on the left and hardest on the right. Bring every dog home for a $50 Walk bonus, or head home early from the pause menu and keep your cash.

Grow the business

Pick a walker, your dogs and one gear item for your Pocket, then head out. Spend what you earn at the Pet shop on new dogs, dog slots and gear. Casey walks first; Mr. B joins when you first finish Cabbagetown, Ada The Beaches and Trista Yonge Street.
