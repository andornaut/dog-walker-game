Walk a pack of dogs through Maplewood. Every bump in the road turns into a choice.

A cozy pixel-art lane runner for your phone. Four zones a walk, about 8 minutes.

Play

Drag or tap to steer, or use the arrow keys.

Every bump, a choice

Walk into a skunk, a puddle or a thief and a card opens: pay up, or upset the owners. Every outcome is on the card, and a bump is never game over.

Nine dogs, nine quirks

Koda the husky speeds up your lane changes, Biscuit the beagle sniffs out tips a lane away, and nervous Penny lunges at other dogs. When a dog's need turns urgent, it pulls the pack toward a hydrant, a lamp post or a water bowl.

Keep the owners happy

Every dog comes with an owner and a row of hearts. Tap a doghouse to hand its owner a treat. Let the hearts hit zero and the owner fires you and takes the dog home.

Pick your path

Every walk starts on Elm Avenue and covers four of Maplewood's six zones. Between zones you pick an upgrade card, then the road forks: easiest on the left, hardest and best paid on the right.

Grow the business

Spend your tips and fees at the Pet shop on new dogs, dog slots and gear, and pocket one gear item for each walk. Casey walks first; Ben, Ada and Trista join when you first finish zones 1, 2 and 3.
