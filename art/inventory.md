# Art Inventory

Every art asset the game needs. Keys are the `public/art/manifest.json` keys. Type is the manifest list: `sprite` (PNG plus Aseprite JSON, tags from frameTags), `image` (PNG), `font` (BMFont) or `sound`.

Priority: **core** is needed for the first build, **later** accepts a placeholder.

`<zone>` is a zone's art id: a Toronto neighborhood's, cabbagetown, high-park, distillery-district, kensington-market, the-beaches or yonge-street, or a Maplewood zone's, snooze-crescent, bark-park, woof-way, sniff-street-shops, paws-promenade or the-long-way-home. The Maplewood keys stay until the game has switched to the Toronto ids, then retire.

### Encounter skins

Each zone's look for the Chase, Snatch and Social encounters, drawn by `art/tools/skins_animals.py`. The ones in play are movers, drawn to the pack's scale with 4-frame motion: the Chase animals in the small dogs' 38×38, the Snatch animals in their own frames, the frisbee and jogger dogs as medium dogs (48×48, drawn 0.82× across) and their owners as road people (48×72). The gate and handbag dogs and their owners are not drawn in play; the script does not build them, and their files stay as they are, unmaintained, the dogs at the 40 px scale, 40×40, the owners at the old 56×84 people size. A Social dog's `run` is `loose-dog`'s gallop, 4 frames at 100ms, side view facing right, in its own frame. Each skin's card illustrations are in the choice cards table, drawn by `art/tools/skin_cards_animals.py`.
