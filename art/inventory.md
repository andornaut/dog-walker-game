# Art Inventory

Every art asset the game needs. Keys are the `public/art/manifest.json` keys. Type is the manifest list: `sprite` (PNG plus Aseprite JSON, tags from frameTags), `image` (PNG), `font` (BMFont) or `sound`.

Priority: **core** is needed for the first build, **later** accepts a placeholder.

`<zone>` is a zone's art id: snooze-crescent, bark-park, woof-way, sniff-street-shops, paws-promenade or the-long-way-home.

### Encounter skins

Each zone's look for the Chase, Snatch and Social encounters, drawn by `art/tools/skins_animals.py`, which no longer builds. Zone skins are not drawn in play, since the game draws only the base looks, and their files stay as they are, unmaintained: the Chase and Social animals at the 40 px scale, 40×40, and the Snatch animals and Social owners at the people's 56×84. A Social dog's `run` is `loose-dog`'s gallop, 4 frames at 100ms, side view facing right, in its 40×40 frame. Each skin's card illustrations are in the choice cards table, drawn by `art/tools/skin_cards_animals.py`.
